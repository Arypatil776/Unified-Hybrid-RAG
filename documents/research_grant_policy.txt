Research Excellence Grant

Purpose:
The Research Excellence Grant supports undergraduate students
participating in research projects.

Eligibility:
- Students must be in their third year.
- Minimum GPA required is 8.0.
- Students must be enrolled in an undergraduate program.
- Students must submit a research proposal.

Deadline:
The application deadline is December 15, 2026.

Required Documents:
- Academic transcript
- Research proposal
- Faculty recommendation letter
